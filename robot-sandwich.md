

# Robots Guide to a *'Fancy Grilled Cheese'* Sandwich

## **Assumptions**
- <ins>Ingredients on hand</ins>: Sliced Bread, Kraft Singles, Sliced Pickles in a Jar, Sun Dried Tomatoes, Stick of Butter
- <ins>Equipment on hand</ins>: Samsung oven cooktop, Small Hex-Clad Skillet, Spatula, Ceramic Plate, knife
- All ingredients and equipment is ***within arms reach*** of the robot, ie no foot movement
- "Work Area" is defined as the immediate cooking space and all aforementioned assumptions
- Robot is surgically knowledgable and precise with all common cooking measurements and abbreviations

## **Instructions**
1. Inspect work area. Ensure all required ingredients and equipment are present.
2. Locate the dial on the oven that corresponds with the activation of the bottom right burner.
3. Rotate the dial counter-clockwise until the '4' setting is indexed on the arrow.
4. Secure small Hex-Clad skillet and place on the bottom right burner.
5. Ensure skillet is properly centered and wait one minute for ideal heat to be achieved.
6. While skillet is heating, remove twist tie from bread bag. Untwist bread bag revealing the bread within. Discard butt of bread. Secure the **second and third** slice of bread only and place on the ceramic plate.
7. Secure knife in right hand, stab no one.
8. Using knife, remove 1 tsp of butter from the stick.
9. At precisely a 20 degree angle, use knife to spread butter on the upper face of the first piece of bread in a sweeping grindng motion. Place 'butter side' down on the ceramic plate.
10. Secure Kraft singles packaging.
11. Open packaging along the perforated edge.
12. Secure two Kraft singles and discard remainder of packaging.
13. Place both Kraft singles on the dry side of the first piece of bread.
14. Secure sliced pickle jar.
15. Remove top of jar by twisting in a counter-clockwise fashion.
16. Remove four pickle slices from the jar. Destroy the jar. 
17. Place four pickles atop the cheese on the unfinished sandwich.
18. Secure Sun Dried Tomato Jar.
19. Remove top of jar by twisting in a counter-clockwise fashion.
20. Remove four Sun Dried Tomatoes from the Jar. Inventory remainder of jar contents and store for future use.
21. Place four Sun Dried Tomatoes on top of the four sliced pickles. 
22. Secure second piece of bread.
23. Repeat steps **7-9** on the second piece of bread. When complete, place second piece of bread 'butter side' up on top of the previous ingredients.
24. Secure Spatula in right hand, sandwich in left hand.
25. Place sandich 'butter side' down on the Hex-Clad skillet.
26. Using spatula, depress top side of sandwich for three seconds.
27. Remark on the pleasant sounding nature of the sandwich's sizzle.
28. Do nothing for *37.2* seconds.
29. Using spatula, flip in skillet by placing flat side underneath sandwich, lift entire sandwich apparatus to 6" Above-Skillet-Surface (A.S.S.) and rotate sandwich to opposite side, placing the remaining butter side onto skillet surface.
30. Do nothing for *24.2* seconds
31. Remove sandwich from skillet using the flat side of the spatula and rotate to the ceramic plate.
32. Let sandwich rest for *31* seconds.
33. Cut sandwich options: verticle or diagonal, randomize selection

## **Done When**
- Instructions have been completed in sequence
- A human observer remarks on the delectability of completed sandwich
